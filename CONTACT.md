# Contact and verification

**DM first. Verification required. No phone calls.**

- X: [@nelvOfficial](https://x.com/nelvOfficial)
- Email: **nelv@nelworks.com**
- GitHub: [q3dresearch](https://github.com/q3dresearch)

## What to send

1. Your name and affiliation.
2. A verifiable institutional, newsroom, regulator, legal-aid, or academic contact.
3. What you want to verify.
4. Why you need entity-level detail.
5. Your public key, if you want encrypted delivery.

## Who gets the full report

- journalists with an editor and a published outlet;
- regulators, auditors, inspectors general, and prosecutors;
- legal-aid, civil-rights, or academic researchers;
- affected providers or their authorized representatives.

## What we do not do

- No anonymous accounts.
- No personal email addresses.
- No phone calls, voice notes, or unscheduled calls.
- No in-person meetings without a trusted intermediary.
- No home addresses or family details.
- No source identities.

## Verification is not an accusation

Verification means we can establish that you are who you say you are and that you have a legitimate
reason to see the file. It does not mean we agree with your conclusion, and it does not transfer the
file for publication without terms.

## Response time

This is a research project, not a newsroom. Expect a delay. A short, specific request with a verifiable
contact is easier to answer than a general request for “everything.”
