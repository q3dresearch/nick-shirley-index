Home health is the larger cluster

48 CMS home health + 26 CMS hospice at one address; 57 CDPH home health + 11 hospice active. California home health rank: 48 at one address, next 27. Aggregate only.

CMS and CDPH do not describe the same population

26 CMS hospices vs 11 CDPH hospice licenses at the building. 22 of 26 CMS hospices have no exact-name CDPH record; four exact name matches and one CCN match. This is a reconciliation gap, not proof of unlicensed operation.

Address concentration

26 CMS hospices at 14545 Friar St vs 3 at the next ZIP address. Address normalization and base rates are in the public aggregate note.

Hospice quality vs Los Angeles County

Cluster median Hospice Care Index 7 vs 8 countywide; per-beneficiary spending $28,393 vs $27,044; visits near death 80% vs 87.5%. One upper-tail provider is above the California 90th percentile on payment and days; name withheld.

What these charts do not show

They do not show that any provider billed for services it did not deliver, that any owner controlled another provider, or that the building is unlicensed. They show a cluster and a set of reconciliation gaps that require records.
