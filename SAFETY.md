# Safety and contact policy

**No phone calls.** Written contact only.

## Why no calls

A phone call gives a stranger:

- your voice;
- your number;
- your time zone and availability;
- a real-time channel to pressure you;
- a recording you may not control.

None of that is necessary to verify a journalist, regulator, or researcher. Use written channels.

## If a call is unavoidable

- Do not use your personal number.
- Use a separate number or a trusted intermediary.
- Schedule it in writing through a verified institutional contact.
- Do not record unless you know the law in your jurisdiction and the other party agrees.
- Keep the call short; do not discuss sources, home addresses, or unverified names.
- Follow up in writing with a summary.

## Contact hygiene

- Use a dedicated email address, not your personal one.
- Use a password manager and two-factor authentication.
- Do not click links from unknown senders.
- Verify institutional email domains independently; a display name is not verification.
- Ask for a public key if the material is sensitive.
- Do not send personal identity documents unless there is a clear legal reason.

## Publication hygiene

- Do not publish home addresses, family members, or phone numbers.
- Use entity addresses, not residences.
- Do not publish a candidate list before verification.
- Keep the working map private.
- Preserve records before any contact that could tip a target.
- Get legal review before naming an unverified entity.

## If you receive a threat

- Preserve the message and headers.
- Do not engage.
- Tell a trusted person.
- Report to the platform and, if there is a credible threat, to local law enforcement.
- Do not publish the threat in a way that reveals your location.

## If you are asked to identify a source

- Do not confirm or deny a source by voice, DM, or email.
- Do not share raw notes.
- Use counsel if a legal demand arrives.

This is a research project, not a law-enforcement agency. It has no subpoena power, no witness
protection, and no ability to guarantee anyone’s safety. Act accordingly.
